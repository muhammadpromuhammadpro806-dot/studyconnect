Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

StudyConnect is a free app where students aged 13 and older find study partners, chat privately, form small study groups and join public study communities. It is run by Muhammad Shafiq, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: .

Who can use StudyConnect

You must be at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Profile: display name, a unique username, and optional country and short bio. You can choose whether you appear in Discover.
  • Content you create: private messages, group messages, public community messages, photos you send in chats, feed posts, comments and likes.
  • Social data: friend requests, friends, blocked students, group memberships and invitations, and the communities you joined.
  • Reports and feedback: when you report something or send a complaint, we store what you send. A report about a message includes a copy of that message (and its photo, if any) so it can be reviewed.
  • Technical data: Google Firebase and Google Cloud process technical information such as IP address and device details to run, secure and protect the service.
  • On your device: theme, notification settings and "read" markers are saved in your device storage.

We do not collect your precise location, phone number, contacts, home address, school name or date of birth. Photos are resized in the app and their hidden metadata (such as location) is removed before sending.

How we use data

  • To provide the app: sign-in, profiles, discovery, friends, chats, groups, communities, feed and notifications inside the app.
  • To keep users safe: reviewing reports, enforcing the Community Guidelines, preventing spam and abuse.
  • To support you and improve the app.

Who can see your information

  • Profile: your display name, username, country and bio are visible to other signed-in users. If you turn off Discover, you do not appear in lists, but someone who knows your exact username can still find you.
  • Private chats and groups: only the people in that chat or group.
  • Feed posts and comments: visible to signed-in users until they expire after 24 hours.
  • Community messages: visible to signed-in users who open that community.
  • Email address: never shown to other students.

We do not sell your personal data. We use Google Firebase and Google Cloud as service providers to store data and run the app; data is currently stored on Google servers in the United States. We may share information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Advertising

StudyConnect is free and is supported by advertising. If ads are shown, they are provided by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers to deliver and measure ads. We never show ads inside conversations. Where required, we ask for your consent and apply age-appropriate ad settings. We will update this policy before any change in how ads work.

How long we keep data

  • Feed posts and comments disappear from the app after 24 hours and are then deleted automatically; deletion from our database can take up to about a day more.
  • Chat messages stay until you delete them, until either friend removes the other (which deletes the chat history for both), until a group is deleted, or until an account is deleted.
  • Reports and feedback are kept for as long as needed for safety, legal and record-keeping reasons.
  • When you delete your account, see "Delete your account" for exactly what is removed and what may remain.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile, change who finds you in Discover, delete your messages and posts, block and report other users.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile > Delete account) or by email. See the "Delete your account" page.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or a correction by emailing .

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and protected by access rules so people can only read what they are allowed to see. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: the app owner can access stored content when needed to review reports or keep the service safe. No online service can be perfectly secure.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions or requests: .

Community Guidelines

Last updated: 6 November 2026

StudyConnect is for students to learn together. Please help keep it kind and safe.

Do

  • Be respectful and encouraging, even when you disagree.
  • Keep posts about learning, study tips and school life.
  • Report anything that makes you uncomfortable and block people who bother you.

Do not

  • Bully, harass, threaten or shame anyone, or post hate against any group.
  • Share sexual or nude content, graphic violence, or content that promotes self-harm, drugs or dangerous acts.
  • Share private details such as home address, phone number or exact school, yours or anyone else's.
  • Pretend to be someone else, or post another person's photo without their permission.
  • Ask for or send personal contact details, photos or favours to people you only know through the app. Adults must not contact minors in any inappropriate way.
  • Spam, advertise, scam, or share links to harmful sites.
  • Share illegal content or help others cheat or break the law.

What happens if rules are broken

Content can be removed and accounts can be limited, suspended or deleted. You can appeal by emailing .

If someone is in danger

StudyConnect is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number and a trusted adult.

Delete your StudyConnect account

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open StudyConnect and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

Option 2: By email

If you can no longer use the app, email from the email address used for your account with the subject Delete my StudyConnect account. Include your username. We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login, profile, username and settings.
  • Your friendships and the chat history with each friend (the history is deleted for both people).
  • Groups you own, with their messages, and your membership in other groups.
  • Your feed posts (with their comments) and your messages in public communities.
  • Your friend requests, invitations, blocks and community memberships.

What may remain

  • Comments you wrote on other people's posts disappear automatically within about a day, when those posts expire.
  • Reports and feedback you sent, and reports made about you, may be kept for safety and legal reasons.
  • Google may keep backup copies for a short period before they are removed.
